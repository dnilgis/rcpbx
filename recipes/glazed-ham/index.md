# Glazed Ham

Holiday centerpiece, sweet and sticky

Serves: 12-15

## Ingredients

- 1 bone-in spiral-sliced ham (8-10 lbs)
- 1 cup brown sugar
- ¼ cup honey
- 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
- ¼ tsp cloves
- ¼ tsp cinnamon

## Steps

1. Place ham cut-side down in roasting pan.
2. Cover tightly with foil. Bake at 250°F until 120°F internal, about 2-2½ hours.
3. Mix glaze ingredients.
4. Remove foil. Increase oven to 350°F.
5. Brush with half the glaze. Bake 15 minutes.
6. Brush with remaining glaze. Bake until 140°F internal and caramelized, 20-30 minutes.
7. Rest 15 minutes.

## Notes

- Spiral ham is already cooked: you are heating and glazing. Heat to 140°F if it was packaged fully cooked in a USDA-inspected plant (check the label), 165°F if not.
- Cut-side down keeps meat moist.
