# Irish Bread

Graham flour, buttermilk, an X on top. No yeast, no waiting.

Prep: 10 min · Cook: 45 min · Makes: 1 round loaf

## Ingredients

- 2 cups graham flour
- 1 cup white flour, plus a lot more for the cloth
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 cups buttermilk, shaken

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Grease a round baking pan.
2. Thoroughly mix the graham flour, white flour, baking soda and salt.
3. Add the buttermilk and mix until all the batter is wet.
4. Flour a pastry cloth with a lot of flour. Turn the dough onto it and sprinkle more flour over the top. Knead just until it holds together, then place it in the pan.
5. Cut an X on top with a knife.
6. Bake 45 minutes, until browned and the bottom sounds hollow when tapped.

## Notes

- The dough is wet and sticky. That's why the card says a lot of flour on the cloth. Use it, and knead only a few turns.
- Graham flour is coarse whole-wheat flour. Plain whole-wheat flour works if you can't find it.
- Soda bread starts rising the moment the buttermilk hits the soda. Get it in the oven right away.
- Best the day it's made. Toast it after that.

## When it goes wrong

- Dense, tough loaf → kneaded too long; a few turns only.
- Flat and spread out → dough too wet for the pan, or sat too long before baking; flour the outside well and bake immediately.
- Soapy or metallic taste → soda not mixed through; whisk the dry ingredients thoroughly first.
