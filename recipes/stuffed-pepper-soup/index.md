# Stuffed Pepper Soup

Every part of a stuffed pepper, none of the stuffing.

Prep: 15 min · Cook: 45 min · Serves: 6

## Ingredients

- 1 lb (450g) ground sirloin
- 1 green bell pepper, chopped
- 1 cup finely diced onion
- 1 can (29 oz / 822g) diced tomatoes, with juice
- 1 can (15 oz / 425g) tomato sauce
- 1 can (14 oz / 400ml) chicken broth
- 1/4 tsp dried thyme
- 1/4 tsp dried sage
- Salt and black pepper, to taste
- 2 cups (475ml) water
- 1 cup (185g) long-grain white rice, uncooked

## Steps

1. Heat a large pot or deep skillet over medium-high. Add the beef and break it up as it cooks, 5-7 minutes, until browned and crumbly with no pink left. Spoon off the fat.
2. Add the green pepper and onion. Cook, stirring, about 5 minutes, until the onion is soft and translucent.
3. Stir in the diced tomatoes, tomato sauce, broth, thyme and sage, and season with salt and pepper. Cover and simmer 30-45 minutes, until the peppers are tender.
4. While it simmers, bring the water and rice to a boil in a saucepan. Cover, drop to medium-low and cook 20-25 minutes, until the rice is tender and the water is absorbed.
5. Stir the rice into the soup, heat through, taste for salt and pepper, and serve.

## Notes

- Leftovers: the rice keeps drinking broth in the fridge, so day-two soup gets thick. Thin it with broth or water when reheating, or store the rice separately and stir it in per bowl.
- Freezes well without the rice, up to 3 months. Cook fresh rice when you reheat.
- Ground sirloin is lean, so there's little fat to drain. With 80/20 beef, drain well before the vegetables go in.
- Shredded cheddar or mozzarella on top is the stuffed-pepper move.

## When it goes wrong

- Tastes flat → under-salted; canned tomatoes and broth vary, so taste and salt at the end, not just the start.
- Peppers still crunchy → simmered too short; give it the full 45 minutes and check a piece.
- Greasy surface → fat not drained after browning; skim with a wide spoon.
- Thick and stodgy leftovers → the rice absorbed the broth; thin with liquid when reheating.
