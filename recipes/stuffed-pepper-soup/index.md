# Stuffed Pepper Soup

Every part of a stuffed pepper, none of the stuffing.

Prep: 15 min · Cook: 40 min · Serves: 6

## Ingredients

- 1 Tbsp olive oil
- 1 lb (450g) ground beef, 85/15
- 1 large yellow onion, diced
- 3 bell peppers (any mix of green, red, yellow), diced, about 3 cups
- 1 1/2 tsp Diamond Crystal kosher salt (3/4 tsp table salt), divided, plus more to taste
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 Tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 can (14.5 oz / 411g) diced tomatoes, with juice
- 1 can (15 oz / 425g) tomato sauce
- 4 cups (950ml) beef broth
- 1 Tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tsp brown sugar
- 3/4 cup (140g) long-grain white rice, uncooked
- 1 1/2 cups (355ml) water, for the rice
- 1 cup (100g) shredded cheddar or mozzarella, for topping
- Chopped parsley, for topping

## Steps

1. Heat oil in a large Dutch oven over medium-high. Add beef and break it up; cook 6-8 minutes, without stirring constantly, until no pink remains and the bottom of the pot has browned bits. Spoon off all but about 1 Tbsp of fat.
2. Add onion, peppers and 1 tsp of the salt. Cook 6-8 minutes, stirring now and then, until the onion is soft and the peppers have lost their raw crunch.
3. Add garlic, tomato paste, oregano, paprika and pepper. Stir 1 minute until the paste darkens to brick red.
4. Pour in diced tomatoes, tomato sauce, broth, Worcestershire and brown sugar. Scrape the bottom of the pot clean. Bring to a boil, then lower to a gentle simmer, partly covered, 20 minutes until the peppers are fully tender.
5. While the soup simmers, cook the rice: bring rice, 1 1/2 cups water and the remaining 1/2 tsp salt to a boil in a small saucepan, cover, drop to low and cook 18 minutes. Off heat, leave covered 5 minutes, then fluff.
6. Taste the soup and adjust salt. A splash of broth thins it if it got too thick.
7. Spoon rice into bowls, ladle soup over, top with cheese and parsley.

## Notes

- Rice stays separate on purpose. Cooked in the pot, it keeps drinking broth in the fridge and day-two soup turns into a casserole.
- Want it all in one pot anyway: stir the raw rice in at the start of the 20-minute simmer with 1 extra cup of broth, and simmer until the rice is tender. Eat it the same day.
- Freezes well without the rice, up to 3 months. Cook fresh rice when you reheat.
- Green peppers taste most like the classic stuffed pepper; red and yellow make it sweeter.

## When it goes wrong

- Tastes flat → under-salted or under-browned beef; salt to taste and let the beef really brown before the vegetables go in.
- Too sharp or acidic → tomatoes vary; add brown sugar 1/2 tsp at a time.
- Greasy surface → fat not drained after browning; skim with a wide spoon or lay a paper towel on the surface for a second.
- Thick and stodgy leftovers → rice was stored in the soup; thin with broth when reheating, and store rice separately next time.
