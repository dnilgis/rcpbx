# Caesar Salad a la Paul Corbin

The card admits it's a little vague. Anchovies, a coddled egg, no bottle.

Prep: 15 min · Serves: 4

## Ingredients

- 1 can anchovies
- 1 clove garlic
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 2 Tbsp Mazola (corn) oil
- 2 Tbsp lemon juice
- 1 coddled egg
- 2 bunches romaine lettuce, washed and dried
- Parmesan cheese, freshly grated
- Croutons

## Steps

1. Coddle the egg: lower it, in the shell, into boiling water for 1 minute, then run it under cold water.
2. In the salad bowl, mash the anchovies and garlic to a fine texture.
3. Add both oils and the lemon juice and stir with a wooden spoon. Crack in the coddled egg and stir until creamy.
4. Add the romaine and sprinkle with Parmesan.
5. Mix gently and add the croutons.

## Notes

- The card lists olive oil and Mazola oil but only mentions olive oil in the steps. Both go in.
- The card doesn't say how to coddle. One minute in boiling water is the standard method: the white just starts to set, the yolk stays raw.
- A coddled egg is mostly raw. For kids, pregnancy, older people or anyone with a weak immune system, use a pasteurized egg.
- The romaine has to be completely dry or the dressing slides off.
- A whole can of anchovies is a lot. That's the point of this one.

## When it goes wrong

- Thin, watery dressing → wet lettuce; spin or towel it dry.
- Gritty anchovy bits → not mashed enough; mash to a paste before the oil goes in.
- Soggy croutons → added too early; they go on last, right before serving.
