# Cinnamon Honey Butter

The Texas Roadhouse butter. Illegal to serve rolls without it.

Prep: 5 min · Cook: 0 min · Makes: about 1 cup

## Ingredients

- 225g (2 sticks) unsalted butter, fully softened (leave it out 2 hours - actually softened)
- 1/4 cup (85g) honey
- 1/4 cup (30g) powdered sugar
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp Diamond Crystal kosher salt

## Steps

1. Beat the softened butter with a hand mixer (or stand mixer, whisk attachment) for 1 minute until creamy.
2. Add honey, powdered sugar, cinnamon, and salt. Beat 2-3 more minutes, scraping down once, until pale, fluffy, and visibly whipped - it should hold soft peaks like frosting.
3. Serve at room temperature. Refrigerate leftovers up to 2 weeks; let it come back to room temp (or re-whip 30 seconds) before serving.

## Notes

- The whip is the recipe. Stirred-together ingredients taste the same but spread like cold spackle - the airiness is the Roadhouse texture.
- Salted butter works: drop the added salt.
- Also correct on: pancakes, cornbread, sweet potatoes, toast, a spoon.

## When it goes wrong

- Grainy → butter wasn't soft enough to emulsify; let it warm and re-whip.
- Separated or greasy → over-whipped in a warm kitchen; chill 10 minutes and whip briefly.
- Too sweet → that's the recipe; halve the powdered sugar next time and it's still right.
