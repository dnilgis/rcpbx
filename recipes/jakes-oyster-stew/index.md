# Jake's Oyster Stew

Oysters, butter, milk. Edges curl, then it's done.

Prep: 5 min · Cook: 15 min · Makes: about 2 1/2 quarts

## Ingredients

- 4 Tbsp flour
- 3 tsp salt
- 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
- 4 Tbsp water
- 1 pint shucked oysters, drained and washed
- 1/2 cup butter
- 2 quarts milk
- Celery salt, to taste

## Steps

1. In a soup kettle, blend the flour, salt, Worcestershire and 4 Tbsp water into a smooth paste.
2. Add the oysters and butter. Simmer over very low heat 3-4 minutes, stirring gently, until the edges of the oysters curl.
3. Add the milk and heat, stirring, until steaming hot. Keep it below a boil.
4. Add celery salt to taste. Serve hot.

## Notes

- Curled edges are the doneness signal. Past that, oysters go rubbery fast, so get the milk in right away.
- Don't let the milk boil. It can scorch on the bottom or break, and the oysters toughen.
- The card drains and washes the oysters. If you want a stronger oyster flavor, strain the liquor and add it with the milk.
- 3 tsp salt is the card's amount for 2 quarts of milk. Start with 2 and taste before the celery salt goes in if your butter is salted.

## When it goes wrong

- Rubbery oysters → cooked too long before the milk; pull at the first curl.
- Grainy or split stew → milk boiled; keep it at a steam, not a bubble.
- Lumps of flour → paste not smooth before the oysters went in; whisk the flour and water first.
