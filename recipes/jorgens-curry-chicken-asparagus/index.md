# Jorgen's Curry Chicken & Asparagus Bake

Smash it, curry it, cheese it, bake it. Over rice.

Prep: 15 min · Cook: 20 min · Serves: 4

## Ingredients

- 2 large boneless skinless chicken breasts, about 1 1/2 lb (680g)
- 1 Tbsp curry powder
- 1 tsp Diamond Crystal kosher salt (1/2 tsp table salt), plus a pinch for the asparagus
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 2 Tbsp olive oil or melted butter, divided
- 1 lb (450g) asparagus, woody ends snapped off
- 1 1/2 cups (170g) shredded cheese (mozzarella, Monterey Jack or cheddar)
- 1 cup (185g) long-grain white rice, uncooked
- 2 cups (475ml) water, for the rice

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Grease a 9x13-inch baking dish with a little of the oil.
2. Start the rice: bring rice, 2 cups water and a pinch of salt to a boil, cover, drop to low and cook 18 minutes. Off heat, leave covered until the chicken is done.
3. Cut each breast in half crosswise. Put the pieces between two sheets of plastic wrap and smash with a meat mallet or heavy pan to an even 1/2 inch thick.
4. Mix curry powder, salt, garlic powder and pepper. Rub the chicken with 1 Tbsp oil, then the spice mix, both sides.
5. Toss asparagus in the dish with the remaining oil and a pinch of salt and spread it out. Lay the chicken on top in a single layer.
6. Bake 15 minutes. Pull the dish, scatter cheese over the chicken, and bake 5 minutes more, until the cheese is melted and the thickest piece reads 165°F (74°C).
7. Rest 5 minutes. Fluff the rice, then serve the chicken and asparagus over it with a spoonful of the pan juices.

## Notes

- Smashing is the point: even thickness means the thin end doesn't dry out before the thick end is done.
- Curry powders differ a lot in heat. Madras is hot, most supermarket blends are mild. Taste yours before the rub.
- Pencil-thin asparagus can go soft by the end; lay it around the chicken instead of under it, or add it at the cheese step.

## When it goes wrong

- Dry chicken → not smashed evenly, or baked past 165°F; pound to one thickness and use a thermometer.
- Raw-tasting, gritty curry → powder sat dry on the meat; rub the oil on first so the spice blooms in the oven.
- Watery dish → asparagus crowded under the chicken; spread it in one layer, or bake in a larger dish.
- Cheese browned before the chicken was done → added too early; it goes on for the last 5 minutes only.
